# Example GmbH: support handbook

Fictional sample document for the office-agent demo and tests.

## Support hours

Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 CET. Outside these hours, incidents marked urgent are handled by the on-call engineer.

## How do I reset my password?

Open the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password", and follow the link sent to your email address. The link is valid for 30 minutes.

## Where can I find the documentation?

The documentation is published at docs.example.com and is updated with every release.
