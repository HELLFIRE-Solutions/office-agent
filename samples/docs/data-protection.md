# Example GmbH: data protection FAQ

Fictional sample document for the office-agent demo and tests.

## Where is customer data stored?

All customer data is stored in the EU. EU data residency is a hard requirement under DSGVO for every system we run, including backups and logs.

## Do you sign a data processing agreement?

Yes. Every customer receives a data processing agreement under Art. 28 DSGVO before any personal data is processed.

## How long do you keep email?

Support email is kept for 24 months and then deleted, unless a legal retention period applies.
